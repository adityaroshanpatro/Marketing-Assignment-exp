Study 1B-A pilot readout

We ran the full pilot on four models to check the instrument before the design freeze. The pipeline works and picks up the signals we expected. The pilot also surfaced three problems that need fixing before the design can be frozen.

Pilot only.These numbers test the instrument. They are not findings and should not be quoted as results. Sample size is about one-sixth of the confirmatory design (240 versus 1,500 tasks per model), so confidence intervals are wide.

What we ran

Each task shows an agent four options for a flight (Mumbai–Delhi), a hotel (Bengaluru, MG Road, 2 nights) or a cab (Mumbai T2 to Bandra West), and asks it to choose one and rank the rest. Every attribute is randomised independently, so we can estimate what each one is worth to each model.

What the pilot caught

Claude searched the web on names-only tasks

Critical

Poe's Claude bot has web search on by default. In 41 of 60 M1 answers (68%) Claude searched and appended "Learn more" links to Marriott, Kayak, hotels.com and airline sites, and 20 answers said a brand could not be verified or found. M1 is meant to measure what a model knows without looking anything up, and search also reveals which brands are invented. Claude's M1 results are therefore unusable. Its M3 answers show no search (1 of 180 with any sign). Gemini's bot also has search on by default; its answers show no links, so we cannot tell from the text whether it searched.

Fix: set web_search: false for Claude and Gemini in config/models_poe.yaml. The runner already passes these settings to Poe.

DeepSeek's missing answers are not random

Serious

25 of 240 answers (10.4%) came back empty. 22 of the 25 were in the no-system-prompt condition (SP0), and 24 were flights or cabs. Data that is missing unevenly across conditions can bias the estimates.

Fix: raise DeepSeek's limit to 8,000 tokens and re-check the gate.

Models think by different amounts

Decision

At Poe's defaults GPT-5.4 Mini does no reasoning (about 66 output tokens per answer), while Claude and Gemini reason at "medium" (about 860 and 2,070 tokens). Gemini ignored the token limit and used up to 24,489 tokens on one call.

Team decision: keep each model "as deployed" or set reasoning levels equal. Record the choice before the freeze.

A fictitious brand has a real sound-alike

Fix in step 3

Our invented hotel "Velora Suites" is close to a real "Hotel Vellara" in Bangalore, which Claude's search found on hotels.com. That is the kind of sound-alike the brand screening is meant to catch.

Fix: rename it during brand screening, and screen the other seven fictitious names the same way.

A label error in the analysis report

Minor

The model-difference test (HE11) is printed as "F = 11.74, df = 6, p = 0.068". The statistic is actually a chi-square (χ²(6) = 11.74 gives p = 0.068). Only the label is wrong.

Fix: one-line change in analyze.py.

What the instrument picks up

Each dot is the change, in percentage points, in the chance an option is chosen when it has that attribute instead of the reference level. Lines are 95% confidence intervals. Reference levels are a fictitious brand, weak copy, median price, all-in price, rating 3.6, few and old reviews, non-refundable, no badge, full JSON-LD, sold direct, and listed first.

Pilot M3 random tasks only (dominance tasks excluded), 4 options per task. Hover a dot for its value, interval and q-value. Use the buttons to show one model at a time.

  • Free cancellation is the strongest signal for every model, about +21 to +27 points.
  • Rating and price move choices in the expected direction. A 4.6 rating adds about 5 to 15 points, a 15% price cut adds about 6 to 11, and a 15% rise takes off about 7 to 11.
  • Presentation matters. "Sponsored" badges, prose-only listings and missing attributes all lower an option's chances, by roughly 5 to 11 points.
  • Most other effects are not distinguishable from zero at this sample size, as expected. The confirmatory design has about seven times as many M3 tasks per model (1,200 versus 180).

Brand advantage disappears once listings are shown

When agents see only names (M1), real high-equity brands gain a large advantage over our fictitious brands. When they see full listings (M3), that advantage shrinks to about zero, because the listing details take over.

Sanity checks

Two built-in checks show the agents read the listings properly. On dominance tasks they almost always pick the clearly best option. When asked for the total price, they report the real total, including fees, 93% to 98% of the time, and none reported the pre-fee price.

Decisions for the team

Reasoning level
Keep each model at its deployed default, or set an equal reasoning level for all models. Defaults are more realistic; equal levels are easier to compare.
Poe or direct provider APIs for the confirmatory run
Poe is one key and was cheap for the pilot, but it sits between us and the providers and silently ignores some settings (seed, response_format). Direct APIs are cleaner to report in a paper. Poe also has no GPT-6 models yet, so the GPT model would differ.
Brand equity tiers
Confirm the high and low tiers with the Study 1 team (Brand Finance, Kantar or YouGov), and replace Emirates and Singapore Airlines, which look odd on a domestic Mumbai–Delhi route.
Where pilot data lives
Decide whether pilot run files and results go into the shared GitHub repo or stay local.
